Priority Sector Lending (PSL) covers categories including agriculture, micro,
small and medium enterprises (MSME), export credit, education, housing, social
infrastructure, and weaker sections. The RBI Co-Lending Model is aimed primarily
at improving the flow of credit to the unserved and underserved priority sectors.
A co-lent loan is classified under PSL only if it meets the eligibility and
end-use conditions of the relevant PSL category, and each partner reflects its
own share of the exposure.
