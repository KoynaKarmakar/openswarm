Under the RBI Co-Lending Model (CLM) for priority sector lending, banks and NBFCs
may jointly lend. The originating NBFC is required to retain a minimum of 20% of
the individual loans (its minimum retained exposure), while the bank funds the
remaining share of up to 80%. The arrangement is governed by a master agreement,
and the co-lending exposure is subject to the priority-sector norms and the
risk-sharing terms agreed between the partners.
