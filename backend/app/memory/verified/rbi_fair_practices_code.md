Under the Fair Practices Code, lenders must disclose all terms — the annualised
interest rate, fees, and the penal charges — in the loan agreement and a
key-fact statement, in a language understood by the borrower. There must be no
discrimination, no undue harassment in recovery, and a grievance-redressal
mechanism with a named officer. Changes to terms must be notified with adequate
notice, and confidentiality of borrower information must be maintained.
