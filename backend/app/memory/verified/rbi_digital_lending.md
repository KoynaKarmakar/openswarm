Under RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines, all loan disbursals and repayments must be
executed directly between the bank accounts of the borrower and the regulated
entity, without any pass-through or pool account of the lending service provider.
Data collected by digital lending apps must be need-based, collected only with the
borrower's explicit prior consent, and stored in India. A key-fact statement and
the annualised percentage rate must be disclosed to the borrower.
