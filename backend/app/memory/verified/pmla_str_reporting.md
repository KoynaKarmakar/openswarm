Reporting entities (banks and NBFCs) must maintain records and file reports with
FIU-IND: a Cash Transaction Report (CTR) for cash transactions above ten lakh
rupees (aggregated monthly), and a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) for any
transaction that gives rise to a reasonable suspicion of money laundering,
irrespective of amount. Attempting to structure transactions to evade reporting,
launder funds, or obscure the source of money is itself a red flag and must be
blocked and reported, not facilitated.
