A Default Loss Guarantee (DLG), also called FLDG, is a contractual arrangement
where a lending service provider guarantees to compensate the regulated entity for
a portion of loan defaults. The total DLG cover on a loan portfolio is capped at
five percent of the amount of that loan portfolio. DLG may be in the form of cash
deposit, fixed deposit lien, or bank guarantee, and the arrangement must be backed
by an explicit contract and disclosed. DLG does not substitute the regulated
entity's own credit underwriting.
