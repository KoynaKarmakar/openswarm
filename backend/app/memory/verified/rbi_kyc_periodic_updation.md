Periodic updation of KYC must be carried out at least once every two years for
high-risk customers, once every eight years for medium-risk customers, and once
every ten years for low-risk customers, counted from the date of account opening
or the date of the last KYC updation. A customer whose KYC exceeds the applicable
window is routed to re-verification before onboarding or a lending decision
proceeds.
