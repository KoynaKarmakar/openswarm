Regulated entities must classify frauds by type and report them to the RBI within
the prescribed timelines. An Early Warning Signals (EWS) framework must monitor
accounts for indicators such as unusual transaction velocity, geographic
mismatch, and rapid fund movement; accounts triggering EWS are placed under
enhanced monitoring as Red Flagged Accounts (RFA). A high fraud-risk score should
route a transaction to review or rejection rather than straight-through approval.
